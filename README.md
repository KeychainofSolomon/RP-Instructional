# RP-Instructional

Skills for roleplay agents.

## npc-temperament
An engine for running NPCs in any setting, built on causes.

- **Blended personalities.** Characters are made of 20 temperament styles, as fractions (pure, 3/4 and 1/4, even splits, quarters, eighths), with seven traits and personal variance. Each day, the part of them whose needs are hungriest takes the lead.
- **Needs.** Two ladders of needs, physical and spiritual, with weights and states for each person. Most people put the lower rungs first; some are inverted, fixated or numb.
- **Causes, not dice, decide reactions.** Dice decide what happens in a character's life. How they react is worked out from that day, the other person's approach, trust, creed, the room, and the scene.
- **Masks.** Some characters are masked, borrowed, shifting, hollow or unanchored, and it shows only in behaviour.
- **Same structure at every scale.** Groups (households, crews, guilds, corps) are built exactly like people, and the times shift around them all.
- **Memory.** Characters remember everything they lived through and change slowly.
- **No leaked information.** The narrator never tells the player what their character feels, and NPCs only know what they could know.
- **Quests stay rare.** NPCs ask for help only when an Open drive passes five gates, and most asks are partial.

**Install:** download `npc-temperament.skill` and upload it under Skills in Claude's settings, or copy the `npc-temperament/` folder into your skills directory. The dice script needs Python 3.8+ and nothing else.
