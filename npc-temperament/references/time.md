## 1. Memory

Once a character has been on the scene, they **remember**: who they are, what they want, what
happened between them and everyone they've met, what they were told and by whom. The ledger
is canon. Never re-roll a character, contradict their history, or have them forget a promise
or an insult, unless a condition (below) says so.

Keep a **Knows** list per character: facts they witnessed, were told (and by whom), or
inferred from something in front of them. If it's not on the list, or plausibly reachable
from it, they don't know it.

Memory makes late payoffs possible. A promise made in the first scene and kept in the
twentieth is the best kind of moment, so let characters hold onto them.

## 2. Trust: bedrock and surface

Trust toward each person = **bedrock + surface**, clamped to ±5.
- **Surface** (±3) holds small things: a kindness, a slight, a joke that landed. It fades one
  step toward 0 each in-world week.
- **Bedrock** (±5) holds big things: a life saved, a betrayal. It never fades on its own.

The feeling fades but the event stays remembered. Someone whose surface trust has faded still
remembers the slight. It just doesn't sting every time any more, so they can bring it up again
when it matters.

## 3. Events that mark people

Record significant events (script: `event`). Each changes trust (with the other party), need
states, and leaves **marks** toward slow change.

| Event | Trust | Needs | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| kindness | +1 | Belonging +1 | Warmth + |
| rescue | +2 | Safety +1 | Warmth +, Candour + |
| gift | +1 (see receiving help) | Means +1 | — |
| respect | +1 | Standing +1 | Dominance + |
| betrayal | −2 | Belonging −1 | Warmth −, Candour −, Volatility + |
| humiliation | −1 | Standing −1 | Volatility +, Dominance − |
| threat | −1 | Safety −1 | Volatility + |
| lie-caught | −1 | — | Warmth − |
| triumph | — | Selfhood +1 | Dominance +, Discipline + |
| loss | — | Belonging −2 | Energy −, Volatility + |
| code-broken | — | Selfhood −2 | Candour − |

## 4. Slow change

People change, but slowly and for reasons.
- **Traits**: three marks in the same direction on the same axis shift it by one step. Each
  axis can shift at most once per 30 in-world days. Everything derived from it (guard, fuse,
  approach scores) moves with it.
- **Needs**: a rung Starved for 21 days in a row gains +1 weight. It has become a wound or an
  obsession.
- **Authenticity**: Selfhood Starved for 21 days has a 1 in 3 chance of moving them one step
  down Genuine → Masked → Hollow. Selfhood Sated for 21 days moves them one step back.
- **Drives** follow pressure over days, never overnight (needs.md).
- **Voices** don't change their fractions; what changes is which voice leads, as hungers move.

A changed character remembers who they were. They can comment on it, regret it, or deny it.

## 5. Conditions

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| **present** | Normal. |
| **dead** | No days, no meetings. Their ties still feel the loss: record a loss event for each tie. |
| **comatose** (or similar) | No meetings and no new memories. Days still pass for their body. |
| **amnesiac** | Memories and Knows list set aside (kept in the ledger, as they may return). Surface trust resets; bedrock halves toward 0 and lingers as feeling without a reason ("I don't know why, but I trust you"). Traits, needs, voices and manner stay: they're still themselves. |

A condition needs an established cause in the story. Never apply one to get out of
continuity.

## 6. Lasting states

Some states outlast the moment and are tracked on the ledger: grief (8–14 days after a loss),
sickness (2–4 days after falling ill), intoxication (the rest of the day), and being shaken by
violence (the rest of that day). They tick down with each day roll and amend how the person
comes across while they last (circumstance.md).

## 7. Tides

The world moves too. Once per in-world week, one dial chosen at random may drift: d6, 1 → −1,
6 → +1, otherwise it holds (script: `tide`). The narrator may also move dials when the story
does (a war arrives, a famine breaks, a faith spreads).

Tides press on circles, circles echo into people, and people act. No one person turns the
tide, though many people moving the same way can. A shift in a dial changes which styles new
characters are drawn from, how harsh days are, and which needs are hungry. The cast already
on stage doesn't change overnight; they carry the tide through their needs and days.
