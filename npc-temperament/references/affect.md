# Affect

How what happens to a person moves them: pain and elation, getting used to things, clarity,
inclinations, and slow change against the anchor of who they are. Everything here arises,
changes and fades. Nothing holds forever.

## 1. Pain and elation

Every recorded event has a valence (pain or elation), a base intensity and a kind:

| Event | Valence | Base | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| rescue | elation | 6 | rescue |
| reunion | elation | 5 | reunion |
| triumph | elation | 4 | success |
| joy | elation | 3 | pleasure |
| kindness, gift, respect | elation | 2 | kindness, gifts, praise |
| betrayal | pain | 6 | betrayal |
| loss | pain | 5 | loss |
| code broken | pain | 5 | shame |
| humiliation | pain | 4 | humiliation |
| threat, witnessed violence | pain | 3 | threat, violence |
| lie caught, fell ill | pain | 2 | betrayal, illness |

**Effective intensity** = base × closeness to whoever it concerns (stranger 0.5, enemy 0.6,
familiar or rival 1, authority 1.2, charge or conceptual 1.5, revered 1.8, beloved 2) ×
sensitivity (1 + Volatility ÷ 3) × (1 − how used to that kind they are). A big event (size 2)
counts half again.

**How it lands depends on processing** (interaction.md §9):

| Processing | Felt now | Presses on who they are | Felt later |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expressive | 1.3 | 0.7 | — |
| Contained | 0.6 | 1.3 (bottled) | — |
| Numb | 0.2 | 1.0 | 0.8, one to three days later |
| Devotional | 0.9 | 0.8 | — |
| Intellectual | 0.7 | 0.7 | — |
| Action | 1.0 | 0.9 | — |
| Caretaking | 1.0 | 1.0 | — |
| Bargaining | 0.8 | 0.9 | 0.4, later |
| Performed (hollow) | 0 | 0.3 | — |

**Feeling** (−10 pain to +10 elation) adds up to ±3 to mood and shows as colour ("weighed down",
"heavy; everything costs effort", "lighter than usual", "bright, a little reckless"). It halves
each day. What the numb didn't feel at the time arrives later, all at once.

## 2. Getting used to things

Each kind of moment has its own familiarity (0 to 0.9). Every time it happens, they get more
used to it: faster for the calm, slower for the volatile. Ten sights of violence in ten days and
the tenth lands at about an eighth of the first. Joy fades the same way.

Familiarity loosens by 0.02 a day without exposure. Forty quiet days and the soldier is
sensitive again. Over a campaign this keeps the same events from hitting with the same force
forever, without making anyone numb for good.

## 3. Clarity

Clarity is a lucid neutrality in the middle of charged moments. It can't come from a single
event, only from a **convergence**:
- at least **three different kinds** of moment within the last week (nine days for the
  disciplined),
- adding up to real weight (8, scaled by sensitivity, since calm people feel less),
- with pain and elation nearly **cancelling**: the net within a **margin of 5** of neutral.

The margin **widens** with discipline (+1 per point above 0), calm (+1 per point of Volatility
below 0) and an inclination toward clarity (up to +3).

When moments genuinely converge, after having been apart for a full window, there is **one
chance**: 5%, plus up to 5 more from an inclination toward clarity, +2 if Discipline ≥ 2, +1 if
Volatility ≤ −1, −2 if Volatility ≥ 2 (never below 1%). Pure suffering never converges.

When it strikes:
- feeling drops to zero, and for one to four days they are **clear**, a circumstance
  (circumstance.md): no fear-driven, raging, petty or showy forms; "unusually clear and
  unhurried; sees what's in front of them"; plain, unhurried speech;
- every kind of moment in the window becomes more familiar;
- it presses Volatility down and Openness and Discipline up, strongly the first time and less
  each time after ("rare for them, and it leaves a trace");
- it feeds an **inclination toward clarity**, which in turn widens the margin, raises the chance,
  and presses discipline and calm. People who keep finding it grow toward it, while pain and
  elation keep doing their work.

Measured: under the same steady mix of pain and joy, a genuine convergence becomes clarity
about 4% of the time for an ordinary person, 3% for a volatile one and 7% for a calm and
disciplined one.

## 4. Inclinations

What someone keeps doing, they become inclined to do.

- **Showing a manner** (every `meet` result) and **repeating an act** (`habit`: drinking,
  praying, fighting, lying, giving, training, carousing, studying, or anything else) feed an
  inclination. It grows by less each time and fades by 0.01 a day without being fed.
- Each repetition presses on the traits behind it: playful and showy manners on Energy,
  dominant ones on Dominance, warm or giving ones on Warmth (cold ones against it), rage and
  fear on Volatility, formal ones on Discipline, covert and petty ones against Candour. Acts
  carry their own (drinking against Discipline, fighting toward Volatility and Dominance,
  praying toward Discipline, lying against Candour).
- **0.3+**: a pull toward it shows as colour.
- **0.5+**: a habit. When nothing in the moment decides strongly, the habit fills the gap ("out
  of habit"), but only within the band it belongs to and never against a circumstance.
- **0.7+**: a compulsion (control 9), which builds and acts like any other.
- Indulgent habits (drinking, gambling, using, carousing) satisfy less over time, and they
  reach for more of it. Strong habits crave: three days without and their Comfort drops.

Measured: a jailer who mocks prisoners daily has the habit within a week, a compulsion within
three, and slightly colder Warmth after forty days. A clerk drinking nightly has a compulsion
by day eleven, craves it on day three without, and the pull is still half there forty days later.

## 5. Change against the anchor

Traits change when pressure on them outweighs their **anchor**, the strength of their underlying
nature:

```
anchor = (3 + half the core value's size) + 1 if Discipline ≥ 2 + 1 if Complete
         × 0.5 when moving back toward their core
         × (1 + half the distance from core) when moving further away
```

Pressure builds from events and repetitions (a third of an event's intensity, scaled by
processing), eases 2% a day, and cancels when pushes go opposite ways. When it outweighs the
anchor, the trait moves one step, at most once a month per trait. An **overwhelming** moment
(intensity at least twice the anchor, such as a child lost or a rescue from certain death)
moves it at once, by one step, even inside that month.

Measured: a person whose core Warmth is +3 survives sixty betrayals by strangers unchanged, but
two by someone they love change them. Under heavy random events (one most days), people change
about one or two traits in four months; in ordinary lives, almost never.

## 6. Cores move too

A trait held away from its core for 90 days becomes part of who they are: the core follows by
one step and the anchor recalibrates around the new self. Whoever they've become can harden,
and can soften again.

## Personal tempo

People are not clocks. Each person carries their own pace, worked out at creation with personal
variance: how long grief runs for them (a loss's usual length times their grief tempo, times the
variance of that particular loss), how fast feelings and habituation fade, and how readily old
memories come back. The mechanics stay the same for everyone; the pace is theirs. An important
memory (a heavy event, a loss, a betrayal, something they took by remembering it) can resurface
on any day, more often on its anniversary and less as it ages, bringing a little of its feeling
back with it. The audit checks that recovery from the same kind of loss varies widely between people.
