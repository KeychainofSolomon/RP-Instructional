## 1. The two ladders

| Rung | Physical ladder | Rung | Spiritual ladder |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Survival** — food, water, warmth, health, their fix | 1 | **Belonging** — someone or some group is theirs |
| 2 | **Safety** — not hurt, not hunted, a door that locks | 2 | **Standing** — respect, recognition, a name that means something |
| 3 | **Means** — money, tools, work, territory | 3 | **Selfhood** — acting on their own judgement; being real to themself |
| 4 | **Comfort** — rest, pleasure, ease, small luxuries | 4 | **Meaning** — purpose, faith, a cause, something bigger than them |

The ladders are separate. A person can be fed and safe yet hollow inside, or starving and
utterly sure of what they live for.

## 2. Weights (0–3)

Start: Survival 2, Safety 2, every other rung 1. Each voice adds 2 × its fraction to the two
rungs it hungers for and takes 1 × its fraction from the one it shrugs off (style table in
personality.md). Round. Then:
- one random rung +1 (a personal hunger no style explains),
- Faith ≥ 2: Meaning +1 · Strangeness ≥ 2: Selfhood +1 · Power ≥ 2: Standing +1,
- Survival and Safety never below 1,
- Hollow: Selfhood and Meaning 0, Belonging at most 1.

A weight-0 rung is never struck by day events: it simply doesn't register for them.

## 3. States

Sated (+1), Steady (0), Wanting (−1), Starved (−2). At creation: two live rungs Wanting, one
Sated, the rest Steady, then the scene presses: Scarcity ≥ 2 → Survival at least Wanting;
Scarcity 3 and a poor station (Hand, Unmoored) → Survival Starved; Violence ≥ 2 → Safety at
least Wanting; Power ≥ 2 and poor → Standing at least Wanting.

**Pressure** of a rung = weight × hunger (Wanting 1, Starved 2). The most pressured rung is
what the person is really about right now.

**Chronic hunger** (mood, every encounter): −1 if any weight ≥ 2 rung is Starved; −2 if
Survival is Starved (standard order) or Selfhood/Meaning is Starved (inverted order).

## 4. Order (d20 at creation)

| d20 | Order | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1–15 | **Standard** | Lower rungs come first. A starved body mutes principle, humour, flattery and easy familiarity (halved) and makes gifts land harder (+1). |
| 16–18 | **Inverted** | Will starve for what they live for. A starved Selfhood or Meaning makes gifts and haggling land half as well and principle land harder. |
| 19 | **Fixated** | Their heaviest rung is never satisfied; it stays at least Wanting whatever happens. |
| 20 | **Numb** | Nothing presses. Their drive is Still. |

If the dominant voice is Zealot, Self-Sacrificing or Predator, Inverted starts at 11.

## 5. The day roll (once per in-world day per character)

1. **Which rung**: pick a rung weighted by weight. What matters to them is what they notice.
2. **How it went**: d20, minus Scarcity for a physical rung, minus Violence too for Safety,
   minus Strangeness for Selfhood (minimum 1): 1–2 → −2 · 3–6 → −1 · 7–14 → 0 · 15–18 → +1 ·
   19–20 → +2.
3. **Cause**: from the table below for that rung, filled with one of *their* ties where the
   entry names one. No cause repeats within seven days.
4. **State** moves by the value (+1/+2 toward Sated, −1/−2 toward Starved).
5. **Circle echo**: if their circle rolled a day, half its value (rounded toward zero) hits
   the same rung for them.
6. **Mood today** = value × sensitivity + echo.
7. Trailing speech: tired (Comfort or Survival Wanting or worse, or a day of no sleep, pain,
   sickness, hunger or exposure) adds 4%; pondering (Openness ≥ 2, a Visionary, Serious or
   Solitary voice leading, or a day that touched their drive) adds 3%; cap 8%. On a hit
   (d100), they trail off with an ellipsis now and then that day.
8. Then: leading voice, contradiction side, openness, temptation (personality.md,
   interaction.md, tables.md).

## 6. Day causes

`{tie}` is filled from their own ties (preferred kinds in brackets), else a plain fallback.

| Rung | Bad (d4) | Good (d4) |
|---|---|---|
| Survival [dependant, kin] | went hungry or thirsty · fell sick · out in the cold or heat too long · supplies ran short for {tie} | ate and drank well · recovered · found supplies for {tie} · slept warm and dry |
| Safety [enemy] | threatened by {tie} · saw violence close by · home broken into · noticed someone watching | a threat from {tie} receded · gained protection · saw a danger dealt with · the street was calm |
| Means [creditor, master] | lost money or work · pressed for payment by {tie} · tools or gear broke · robbed or cheated | got paid · good deal · eased a debt with {tie} · a new opportunity |
| Comfort [place] | slept badly or in pain · went without a relied-on pleasure · {tie} spoiled or disturbed · bored to the bone | indulged a pleasure · finally rested · a small gift · a beautiful moment at {tie} |
| Belonging [kin, lover, friend, group, dependant] | fought with {tie} · no word from {tie} · left out · a lonely anniversary | good time with {tie} · welcomed in · made up with {tie} · word from {tie} |
| Standing [group, rival, master] | humiliated in front of {tie} · passed over · watched {tie} get ahead · a rumour about them | praised in public · recognised by {tie} · got the better of {tie} · asked for advice |
| Selfhood [master, rival, mentor, group] | made to act against their own code · {tie} overrode their judgement · failed their own standard · saw what they've become | a hard choice made their own way · stood up to {tie} · finished something on their own terms · felt like themself again |
| Meaning [cause] | doubted {tie} · a hoped-for sign came to nothing · let down by {tie} · witnessed something cruel and pointless | a sign confirmed {tie} · progress in {tie} · kept a ritual or vow · glimpsed a purpose |

A 0 result is an ordinary day (busy, dull, weather, routine): note the detail anyway.

## 7. Drives

The drive points at the **most pressured rung** at creation (ties go to the lower rung, or the
higher one if inverted). Its type is rolled d20: 1 **Still** (1–3 if Complete; always if Numb),
2–15 **Inward**, 16–20 **Open**. Open drives are **Partial** unless d6 = 6 on a physical,
Belonging or Standing rung, which makes them **Full**. Selfhood and Meaning drives are always
Partial: an outsider can help with a piece, never the heart.

| Rung | Inward (no outsider can give it) | Open (an outsider could help) |
|---|---|---|
| Survival | to stop fearing hunger · to beat a dependency · to accept a failing body · to stop hoarding | steady food or water · medicine for {tie} or themself · proper shelter · a cure |
| Safety | to feel safe at home · to stop flinching · to forgive themself for running · to stop waiting for the knock | to be rid of {tie} · protection for someone they love · to get out · to clear their name with the authorities |
| Means | to stop measuring life in money · peace with having failed · to stop envying those who have more · to feel they've earned enough | clear a debt with {tie} · recover stolen goods · land a deal or job · a permit, tool or licence |
| Comfort | to enjoy anything again · to stop needing a vice · to rest without guilt · to feel at home | a rare pleasure · fix up {tie} · a supply of their vice · a real day off |
| Belonging | to be loved back · to grieve {tie} · forgiveness from someone gone · to stop being the outsider | find {tie} · get word to {tie} · be accepted by {tie} · bring {tie} home |
| Standing | to deserve their position · to stop needing approval · to outgrow a disgrace · to be seen as {tie} never saw them | recognition from {tie} · restore their name · beat {tie} at something · proof of worth (a commission, title, contract) |
| Selfhood | to know who they really are · to stop lying to themself · peace with a choice · to stop being what {tie} made them | free of an oath, contract or owner · a record of who they were · out from under {tie} · a chance to act on their own judgement |
| Meaning | to believe again · to know if they were right · to matter · something they can't name | a relic, text or key to {tie} · reach a place that matters · finish a work begun for {tie} · witness proof |

A **Hollow** character never has a drive resting on Belonging, Selfhood or Meaning: it moves to
their most pressured physical rung or Standing, and nothing in it speaks of love or grief.
This applies the moment they become Hollow, whether at creation or by drifting.

Drives move slowly. When the day roll shows pressure has clearly moved to another rung
(weight × hunger ≥ 4 there), consider shifting the drive over the following days, never
overnight.
