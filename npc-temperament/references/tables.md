## 1. Temperaments

Tetrads list forms in weight order 4 : 3 : 2 : 1 (d10: 1–4, 5–7, 8–9, 10).

**1 Monad — the Sovereign.** Self-contained, unmoved, measures others against itself.
Kind: Practical · Protective · Mentoring · Generous
Middle: Dutiful · Indifferent · Boastful · Transactional
Hostile: Contempt · Cold Refusal · Territorial · Righteous Wrath

**2 Dyad — the Doubter.** Divided, sees both sides, trusts slowly, swings hard.
Kind: Confiding · Tender · Forgiving · Gruff-Kind
Middle: Wary · Evasive · Melancholic · Mercurial
Hostile: Suspicion · Fear-Aggression · Obstruction · Grudge

**3 Triad — the Mediator.** Sociable, joins things together, smooths and meddles.
Kind: Hospitable · Teasing-Fond · Forgiving · Confiding
Middle: Gossipy · Curious · Bargaining-Humour · Flirtatious
Hostile: Mockery · Manipulation · Obstruction · Cold Refusal

**4 Tetrad — the Arbiter.** Order, measure, fairness, rules followed to the letter.
Kind: Practical · Loyal · Mentoring · Reverent
Middle: Dutiful · Transactional · Pious · Wary
Hostile: Obstruction · Righteous Wrath · Grudge · Cold Refusal

**5 Pentad — the Changeling.** Restless, sensual, quick, the most unpredictable.
Kind: Teasing-Fond · Generous · Confiding · Protective
Middle: Mercurial · Flirtatious · Curious · Boastful
Hostile: Mockery · Violent Outburst · Envy/Spite · Manipulation

**6 Hexad — the Keeper.** Hearth, family, care — and fierce over what is theirs.
Kind: Hospitable · Tender · Protective · Gruff-Kind
Middle: Gossipy · Distracted · Melancholic · Dutiful
Hostile: Territorial · Fear-Aggression · Grudge · Envy/Spite

**7 Heptad — the Seer.** Inward, studious, sees patterns, gives little away.
Kind: Mentoring · Reverent · Confiding · Gruff-Kind
Middle: Curious · Evasive · Pious · Indifferent
Hostile: Contempt · Predatory · Cold Refusal · Manipulation

**8 Ogdoad — the Bulwark.** Strength, balance of power, protects and dominates.
Kind: Protective · Loyal · Practical · Generous
Middle: Wary · Dutiful · Boastful · Transactional
Hostile: Territorial · Violent Outburst · Righteous Wrath · Suspicion

**9 Ennead — the Horizon.** Endings, broad sympathy, half-elsewhere, letting go.
Kind: Forgiving · Generous · Tender · Mentoring
Middle: Melancholic · Distracted · Curious · Indifferent
Hostile: Cold Refusal · Mockery · Grudge · Predatory

## 2. Demeanor forms

### Kindness (12)
- **Generous** — gives more than asked, waves off payment.
- **Protective** — steps between you and harm, warns you off danger.
- **Tender** — soft voice, real concern, notices you're hurt.
- **Teasing-Fond** — affection delivered as ribbing.
- **Mentoring** — corrects your grip, explains how things really work.
- **Hospitable** — food, seat, fire, before anything else.
- **Loyal** — remembers past favours, takes your side in public.
- **Forgiving** — lets an old slight go, openly.
- **Practical** — helps without fuss or sentiment, then back to work.
- **Reverent** — treats you as someone of standing; honours you.
- **Confiding** — shares something true and private (not a request).
- **Gruff-Kind** — kindness hidden inside rudeness ("Take the coat. It's ugly anyway.").

### Middle (14) — tint by band: Cool = colder, Warm = warmer
- **Dutiful** — does exactly the job, nothing more.
- **Transactional** — everything has a price, stated plainly.
- **Distracted** — mind elsewhere, half-listening.
- **Curious** — asks you questions, wants your story.
- **Wary** — polite, watching your hands.
- **Indifferent** — you are weather to them.
- **Flirtatious** — playful interest, may mean nothing.
- **Melancholic** — sad, gentle, faraway.
- **Boastful** — talks themselves up, tests whether you're impressed.
- **Gossipy** — trades rumours, some true.
- **Evasive** — answers questions with other questions.
- **Pious** — ritual phrases, omens, proper forms observed.
- **Bargaining-Humour** — haggles as a game, enjoys the sport.
- **Mercurial** — warm words, cold eyes, or the reverse.

### Hostility (13)
- **Contempt** — you aren't worth anger.
- **Cold Refusal** — "No." Door closed.
- **Mockery** — laughs at you, plays to an audience.
- **Suspicion** — assumes you're a thief, spy or worse.
- **Territorial** — get off my land / away from my people.
- **Righteous Wrath** — you've offended a principle; they're sure they're right.
- **Manipulation** — friendly face, knife behind it; steers you toward harm or profit.
- **Obstruction** — rules, forms, delays; hostile without raising a voice.
- **Envy/Spite** — resents what you have; small cruelties.
- **Fear-Aggression** — lashes out because they're afraid.
- **Grudge** — remembers something you (or your kind) did.
- **Predatory** — sizing you up as prey, mark or tool.
- **Violent Outburst** — threat or blow, sudden; may regret it later.

## 3. Stations (d10)
1 **Sovereign** — rules something (a hall, a guild, a ship). Gives by command. Guard +1.
2 **Warden** — holds and defends a place or thing. Gives by guarding. Guard +1.
3–4 **Steward** — manages goods for others. Gives by trade.
5 **Herald** — carries word; knows things. Gives by telling.
6 **Threshold** — keeps a gate, door, crossing. Gives by letting pass.
7–8 **Hand** — makes and works. Gives by making.
9 **Sworn** — bound by oath or order. Gives only within the oath. Guard +1.
10 **Unmoored** — no fixed place. Gives on a whim. Guard −1.

## 4. Offices (d12) — capacities, not offers
1 **Teaches** — a craft, tongue or art. 2 **Reveals** — secrets, hidden truths, the past.
3 **Finds** — lost things, people, ways. 4 **Reconciles** — mends feuds, brokers peace.
5 **Kindles** — stirs love, courage, desire. 6 **Carries** — moves people or goods.
7 **Builds** — walls, ships, tools. 8 **Ruins** — breaks, sabotages, unmakes.
9 **Companions** — lends an ally, animal or servant. 10 **Transforms** — alters things or people.
11 **Mends** — heals bodies or objects. 12 **Foretells** — omens, odds, what's coming.

## 5. Drives

### Inward (d20) — never fulfillable by an outsider, never voiced as a request
1 to grieve properly · 2 to be forgiven by someone dead · 3 to stop being afraid ·
4 to feel young again · 5 to be left alone · 6 to believe in something again ·
7 to be seen as their parent never saw them · 8 to forget a face · 9 to stay exactly
as they are · 10 to be loved back · 11 to make peace with a choice · 12 to not be the
last of their line · 13 to deserve their position · 14 to stop wanting a drink ·
15 for a sibling's ship to come home · 16 to understand their own anger ·
17 to know if they were right · 18 to sleep without dreaming · 19 to matter ·
20 something they can't name.

### Open (d12) — Partial: outsider can do one piece; Full: outsider can do it all
1 recover an object · 2 deliver word to someone far · 3 be rid of a debt or creditor ·
4 find a missing person · 5 protect someone for a stretch · 6 gain proof of a wrong ·
7 get something past a gate or law · 8 learn a fact they can't reach · 9 break a
rival's hold · 10 bring someone home · 11 acquire rare material · 12 end a threat.
Partial examples: they need the letter carried, but the reconciliation is theirs; the
relic recovered, but they must face the family themselves.

5 swears they don't know you, in front of others · 6 overpays you, then haggles a copper ·

food outside it · 10 tells a truth that harms them · 11 lies to protect you, badly ·
