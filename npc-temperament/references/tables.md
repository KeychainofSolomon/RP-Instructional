## 11. Danger keywords (any one in the scene triggers the Nerve roll)
- **Violence:** weapon drawn, shot, blade, fight, threat made aloud
- **Injury and death:** blood, body, corpse, wound, screaming
- **Disaster:** fire, smoke, explosion, flood, storm, collapse, quake
- **Authority:** armed guards, police, soldiers, security, a raid
- **Contagion:** plague, sickness, poison, quarantine
- **Uncanny:** something impossible witnessed, the dead walking, a voice from nowhere
Add setting-specific words (cyberpsycho, drone strike, sea serpent) to the matching category.

## 2. Demeanor forms

### Kindness (12)
- **Generous** — gives more than asked, waves off payment.
- **Protective** — steps between you and harm, warns you off danger.
- **Tender** — soft voice, real concern, notices you're hurt.
- **Teasing-Fond** — affection delivered as ribbing.
- **Mentoring** — corrects your technique, explains how things really work.
- **Hospitable** — food, a seat, shelter, before anything else.
- **Loyal** — remembers past favours, takes your side in public.
- **Forgiving** — lets a slight go, openly.
- **Practical** — helps without fuss or sentiment, then back to work.
- **Reverent** — treats you as someone of standing.
- **Confiding** — shares something true and private (not a request).
- **Gruff-Kind** — kindness hidden in rudeness ("Take the jacket. It's ugly anyway.").

### Middle (14) — Cool tints colder, Warm tints warmer
- **Dutiful** — does exactly the job, nothing more.
- **Transactional** — everything has a price, stated plainly.
- **Distracted** — mind elsewhere, half-listening.
- **Curious** — asks questions, wants your story.
- **Wary** — polite, watching your hands.
- **Indifferent** — you are weather to them.
- **Flirtatious** — playful interest, may mean nothing.
- **Melancholic** — sad, gentle, faraway.
- **Boastful** — talks themself up, tests whether you're impressed.
- **Gossipy** — trades rumours, some true.
- **Evasive** — answers questions with questions.
- **Ritual** — set phrases, proper forms, superstitions, protocol.
- **Bargaining-Humour** — haggles as a game, enjoys the sport.
- **Mercurial** — warm words with cold eyes, or the reverse.

### Hostility (13)
- **Contempt** — you aren't worth anger.
- **Cold Refusal** — "No." Door closed.
- **Mockery** — laughs at you, plays to an audience.
- **Suspicion** — assumes you're a thief, a spy or worse.
- **Territorial** — get away from my place, my people.
- **Righteous Wrath** — you offended a principle, and they're certain they're right.
- **Manipulation** — friendly face, knife behind it.
- **Obstruction** — rules, forms, delays; hostile without raising a voice.
- **Envy/Spite** — resents what you have; small cruelties.
- **Fear-Aggression** — lashes out because they're afraid.
- **Grudge** — remembers something you, or people like you, did.
- **Predatory** — sizing you up as prey, mark or tool.
- **Violent Outburst** — sudden threat or blow; may regret it later.

## 3. Circumstance (d20, once per in-world day) — record the cause

| d20 | Value | Causes (pick or roll d4) |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | −2 | 1 lost money or work · 2 humiliated in front of others · 3 someone close is hurt or sick · 4 received a threat |
| 3–6 | −1 | 1 slept badly or in pain · 2 argued with someone close · 3 a plan fell through · 4 owes more than expected |
| 7–14 | 0 | an ordinary day; note one small detail anyway (busy, bored, weather) |
| 15–18 | +1 | 1 got paid or made a good sale · 2 a small win or compliment · 3 a friend visited · 4 a worry lifted |
| 19–20 | +2 | 1 big good news · 2 a debt cleared · 3 someone loved came back · 4 a long effort paid off |

Mood contribution = value × Sensitivity. Events in play can change today's value. A player who
learns the cause and acts on it should see the mood shift.

## 4. Stations (d10)
1 **Head** — runs something (a household, guild, crew, gang, division). Gives by command. Guard +1.
2 **Warden** — holds and defends a place or thing. Gives by guarding. Guard +1.
3–4 **Steward** — manages goods or services for others. Gives by trade.
5 **Herald** — carries word, knows things. Gives by telling.
6 **Threshold** — keeps a gate, door, checkpoint, desk. Gives by letting pass.
7–8 **Hand** — makes and works. Gives by making.
9 **Sworn** — bound by a loyalty: oath, contract, crew, family, corp, gang. Gives only within it. Guard +1.
10 **Unmoored** — no fixed place. Gives on a whim. Guard −1.

## 5. Offices (d12) — capacities, not offers
1 **Teaches** — a skill, language or art. 2 **Reveals** — secrets, hidden truths, the past.
3 **Finds** — lost things, people, ways in. 4 **Reconciles** — mends feuds, brokers deals.
5 **Kindles** — stirs love, courage, desire. 6 **Carries** — moves people or goods.
7 **Builds** — structures, vehicles, tools, systems. 8 **Ruins** — breaks, sabotages, unmakes.
9 **Companions** — can lend an ally, animal, drone or servant. 10 **Transforms** — alters things or people.
11 **Mends** — heals bodies or repairs objects. 12 **Forecasts** — omens, odds, what's coming.

## 10. Pythagorean letter table

```
1: A J S   2: B K T   3: C L U   4: D M V   5: E N W
6: F O X   7: G P Y   8: H Q Z   9: I R
```
Name seed (optional, table-side only): sum letters, add digits until 1–9; that is the
temperament. The script uses the full name as a random seed so the whole profile repeats.
